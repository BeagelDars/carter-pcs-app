Carter Ryan Smith, better known online as Carterpcs, is an American technology content creator, internet personality, and PC building expert. Rising to prominence during the early 2020s, he is widely recognized for his fast-paced, highly energetic short-form videos on TikTok, Instagram, and YouTube, where he reviews computer hardware, roasts poorly-priced prebuilt gaming PCs, and advises novices on building custom computer systems.

Early Life & Background

Carter Smith was born on August 8, 2005, in Monroe, Michigan. Developing an early fascination with consumer electronics, computer hardware, and video editing, he began experimenting with PC component pricing and benchmark optimization during his early teenage years.

Rise to Prominence

Smith launched his online career under the moniker @carterpcs on TikTok, where his unorthodox delivery and accessible advice on budget PC building quickly gained traction. One of his earliest viral videos warned viewers about the dangers and misconceptions surrounding improper thermal paste application. He rapidly expanded his reach by curating guides on constructing fully functional gaming PCs for under $400.

As his audience scaled across TikTok, Smith transitioned to long-form and serialized content on YouTube (Carter Tech and CarterPCs channels), where he covers tech industry news, benchmarks high-end GPUs, and reacts to subscriber setup submissions.

Content Style & Tropes

CarterPCs is distinguished by several signature elements across his media production:

  • High Energy Delivery: Eschewing dry, academic hardware reviews in favor of rapid pacing and dynamic jump cuts.
  • Anti-Corporate Skepticism: Frequently cautioning consumers against overpriced prebuilt systems marketed by mainstream boutique PC vendors.
  • Setup Roasts: An interactive segment where he critiques subscriber cable management, monitor placement, and excessive RGB lighting configurations.
  • The Thermal Paste Debate: Frequently referencing thermal paste quantities and application methods as a running community joke.

Cultural Impact

With a combined following exceeding 11 million across platforms, Carterpcs is credited by media analysts with introducing an entire generation of younger demographics to custom PC building, hardware literacy, and consumer advocacy within the gaming hardware ecosystem.
